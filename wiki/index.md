# Stone Age Clans wiki

Every word here is a **Draft**. A draft is the sentence already written in the two dictionary files. It is not locked.

Nothing on this site replaces `bible/game_dictionary.md` until you confirm that page.

## How a page is marked

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Written from the docs. Waiting on you. |
| **Confirmed** | You said the sentence is right, or you replaced it. |
| **Retired** | You said this word is not used. |

**Whose word** is either **Yours** (game language) or **Sim name** (a name from the write-ups).
