# STEAL / TAKE HERD

**Status:** Draft — **player verb planned**; NPC **theft** motive **shipped** (different UI label, same idea)

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

**Player (planned):** On an enemy claim menu, **TAKE HERD** — use **cordage** on **claimed** herdables, bond them, and lead them out like a wild herd. Not fighting the flock for sport.

**NPC (today):** **Theft** motive when a neighbor **stole** your woman or animal (or you hold a grudge target). Goals **take_women** / **take_animals** — may need to **knock down** the hut/farm/dairy that shelters them first (`bible/raid.md`).

## What it is not

- Not [LOOT / TAKE GOODS](loot-take-goods.md) (inventories).
- Not [herd steal](herd-steal.md) on a **wild** animal in the brush (influence contest). STEAL here is **raid / cordage** on **claimed** targets.

## From the bible

- `earlygame_vision.md`: cordage bond on enemy **claimed** herdable; peace walk cannot take claimed without it.
- `raid.md`: theft motive, building must fall before penned targets are herdable.
