# Herd

**Status:** Draft

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

Leading herdables (women, sheep, goats). They can be stolen, or join the clan when brought inside the claim. Not used for cavemen.

## What it is not

Not Follow. Follow is the order for clansmen.
