# Clansman

**Status:** Draft

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

A caveman who belongs to a clan. The word stresses membership and jobs (work, defend, follow, raid). Women and herd animals are not clansmen.

## What it is not

Not a woman, and not a herd animal, even when those are part of the clan.

## Doc fight

gdd.md still says clansmen are only surplus babies turned into an army. The current dictionary meaning is any male in the clan.
