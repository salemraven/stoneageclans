# Predator

**Status:** Draft

## Definition

Hostile wild NPC (wolf, and sometimes mammoth). Not herdable. Fights or hunts.

## Doc fight

Mammoth is also listed as hunt prey (meat) in the early-game vision and the Area of Hunt notes. The docs have not picked one role.
