# Campfire

**Status:** Draft

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

Tier 1. Small movable land claim. Nomad home. Radius 250 px. Up to 3 Living Huts. You can abandon camp and move without disbanding.

## Doc fight

An old snapshot said the campfire has no clan brain and 6 storage slots. The Sep 2026 nomad doc says it does have a clan brain (nomadic mode) and 20 slots.
