# Follow

**Status:** Draft

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

Order for clansmen: stay with you or a leader, in formation, for travel and combat. You break it. It is not stolen like a herd.

## What it is not

Not Herd.

## Doc fight

Older notes treated Follow as the clansman’s state. The current dictionary says Follow is a stance. The squad itself is a party.
