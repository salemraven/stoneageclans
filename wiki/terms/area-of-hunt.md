# Area of Hunt

**Status:** Draft

**Whose word:** Sim name

## Definition

A ring on the flag wider than the claim. Counts prey (deer, mammoth) for the AI hunt. Herdables in that ring are still a search job, not a hunt. The campfire has no Area of Hunt.
