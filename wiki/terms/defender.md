# Defender

**Status:** Draft

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

A clansman assigned to guard the claim edge (flag border or campfire edge). Not anyone who is fighting.

## What it is not

Not Guard. Guard is a moving party stance. A defender stays on the border.
