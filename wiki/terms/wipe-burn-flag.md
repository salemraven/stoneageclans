# WIPE / BURN FLAG

**Status:** Draft — player verb **planned**; flag destruction **shipped**

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

**Player (planned):** **BURN FLAG** on an enemy claim — destroy their **land claim** after loot rules. That triggers a **territory wipe** (buildings and stored goods gone; herdables scatter wild).

**Not** the default end of every NPC raid — most NPC raids **retreat** when a narrower **goal** finishes (`raid.md`).
