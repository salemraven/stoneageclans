# Territory wipe

**Status:** Draft (from bible)

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

When a clan’s **flag (land claim)** is destroyed: **inventories and buildings** on that territory are gone. **Women and herd animals** go **wild** (`become_wild()`). **Babies** despawn with the claim (today). **Loot first** when raiding — smashing the flag deletes what you did not take.

**Planned:** surviving male fighters become **wild cavemen** / exile pipeline — not fully shipped.

## What it is not

- Not the same as an NPC raid **retreating** after food or weaken — that leaves the enemy claim standing.
