# Morale bar (fighter)

**Status:** Draft (planned — see bible)

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

Each **clansman’s** personal morale bar (0–100). Crossing a **flight line** on that bar makes him break and run. Skills and traits move the line.

## What it is not

- Not the **ClanBrain** morale bar. Clan mood is [Morale bar (ClanBrain)](morale-bar-clan.md).
