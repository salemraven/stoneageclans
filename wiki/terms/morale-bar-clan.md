# Morale bar (ClanBrain)

**Status:** Draft (planned)

**Whose word:** Yours

## Definition

A **clan-level** mood meter on the **ClanBrain**. It tracks how bold, united, or shaky the tribe feels for **strategy** (raid, defend, hunt, future defection), not one person’s fight-or-flight.

## What it is not

- Not the per-fighter morale / flight bar in `morale_bar_flight.md`. That bar is on each clansman in combat. This bar is on the **brain**.
